# 002 - Design, palette and guides

> **TL;DR** — ffly.app was redesigned on 2026-10-05 around a night route map and a boarding-pass explainer of Free vs Pro. It uses only the icon's colours (navy, gold, red, white, black). `/guides` holds SEO articles written through `seo/`. When you add a page, use the palette tokens in `global.css`. To add an article, follow the `seo/` pipeline.

**Prerequisites**: [001 - Deployment](001-deployment.md)
**Related**: ios-ffly `docs/specs/014-night-gold-palette.md` (the same palette in the app)

## Overview
The owner wanted the site to stop looking AI-generated and to stop leaking implementation details. It now says what ffly is worth and what the offer is.
- The copy names values: money, time, sleep and fit.
- The offer is 3 free searches, and Pro shows every route in full.
- Mechanics are gone: city limits, must/maybe, background search, fare counts.
- `scripts/check-legal.mjs` fails the build if those words come back on a non-legal page.

## Palette (owner rule: only the icon's colours and their tints/shades)
| Token | Light | Dark | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| navy | `#071230` / ink `#0B1530` | bg `#040A1C`, raised `#0F1C42` | text, surfaces, map panel |
| gold | `#F2C14E` (fills only) | `#F2C14E` (accent and text) | CTA fills with navy text, underlines, route |
| red | `#D93A2B` | `#FF6B5E` | stamp, errors, attention |
| neutrals | navy tints `#F4F6FB`, `#E3E8F2`, `#5A6482` | `#A7B0CC` | backgrounds, secondary text |

Gold is never darkened: a dark gold reads as brown, and the owner rejected it. In light mode, gold never appears as text. The hero map is always a night map, in both themes.

## Components
| Piece | File | Notes |

| Route map | `src/components/RouteMap.astro` | Europe coastline from Natural Earth, generated by `scripts/europe-map.py` into `src/data/europe-map.json`. Directed, numbered dotted legs. A white fly flies the loop with `<animateMotion rotate="auto">` and waits at each city for a time that grows with its nights. Under reduced motion it is parked. No client JS. |

## SEO pipeline (`seo/`)
- `product.md` is the fence for what the product does and doesn't do. `experience.md` holds the owner's first-person facts and the measured example searches. `banned.md` lists anti-slop phrases. `keywords.md` and `clusters.md` hold the backlog. `published.md` lists live URLs.
- Raw results of each example search are kept in `seo/searches/`.
- Guides quote only dated example searches, each with its caveat (CLAUDE.md, copy rules).
- The first two guides scored 94 and 92 from an independent judge. The next topics are "Ryanair multi city" and "Wizz Air multi city".
