# Product definition — ffly

Source of truth: ffly-site `src/app.ts`, ios-ffly `fastlane/metadata/en-US`. Update in the same commit as a product change.

## One-line pitch
ffly finds the order and dates that make a multi-city trip cost the least, with sensible flight times. For people who want to see several cities in one trip without overpaying or losing sleep.

## Platforms
- iOS: yes (iPhone, iOS 17 or later; iPad layouts exist)
- Android: no
- Web: yes, a free search at ffly.app/search (once a day per network; shows the best route's cities, nights and total)

## Pricing
- Free: 3 searches in the app. Shows the cities in the cheapest order, the nights in each and the total price. Other routes show only price and city count.
- ffly Pro: every route in full (dates, flight times, airlines, booking links) and no 3-search limit. Weekly or yearly subscription, or a one-time Lifetime purchase. Priced in the app in local currency. Never quote a price. No free trial.

## Features that EXIST
- Pick a start, one or more places to finish, and the cities to visit (each a must or a maybe; a maybe is dropped when leaving it out makes the trip cheaper). Use the word "must/maybe" only inside articles that explain how to plan, never as a headline claim.
- A travel window and nights per city (a minimum and a maximum).
- What matters most: Best schedule, Balanced or Cheapest. Routes are ranked by that choice.
- Favours sensible flight times over pre-dawn wake-ups and midnight landings. Counts a midnight landing as the hotel night it costs you, even on Cheapest.
- Direct flights only, or a cap on connections. Pin a city to fixed days (e.g. a concert).
- Shows early starts, late arrivals and daylight in each city on routes that have them (Pro shows details).
- Fares from Ryanair, Wizz Air, airBaltic, Volotea and more. You book each flight on the airline's site or a booking site.
- Notifies you when routes are ready. Up to 20 saved trips.
- Pro: exclude a sold-out flight and plan again.

## Features that DO NOT exist (the hallucination fence)
- Booking inside the app; selling tickets; being a travel agent
- Hotels, trains, buses, car hire, packages
- Android app
- Price alerts / fare tracking over time
- Bag fees in the total (fares may not include baggage)
- Accounts, sign-in, sharing trips with friends
- "Unlimited" searches
- Any airline partnership or affiliation

## Things we never claim
- A guaranteed cheapest price, a saving the search did not measure, "always", "best app"
- That fares are live or exact (they are indicative; confirm where you book)
- Medical benefits of sleep
- That one priority is the right way to travel
