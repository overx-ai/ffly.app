## Post

{opening line}

I built ffly, a multi-city trip planner, after planning a one-month trip on a short visa. Everyone says the order of your cities changes the price, but I couldn't find anyone who had tested it.

So on 6 November 2026 I ran eight searches: four European cities each, round trip, February 2027, 3,261 fares compared.

What I found:

• The cheapest and dearest orders shown were 14% to 65% apart, with a median of 30%.
• Those routes also flew on different dates, so that isn't order alone.
• Only two pairs flew on identical days. They differed by €1 and €28, and even they used different airports.
• 16 pairs were exact reversals. The median gap was about 6%, among the cheapest routes shown.

So city order can change a multi-city flight price, but the dates usually move with it. List your cities and a range of nights, then compare whole routes before you fix the order.

Limits: eight searches, one winter month, flights only, some fares last seen a few days earlier, and each search showed 4 to 8 of the 24 possible orders. These aren't prices you'll get.

Full results: https://ffly.app/guides/does-city-order-change-flight-price
