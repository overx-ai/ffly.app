# Anti-slop patterns: dansk (da)

Loaded with `banned.md` for every `--lang da` article (Step 6). Created 2026-11-03. One line per new pattern; remove or rewrite, never soften.

## Hype words
ultimativ, den ultimative guide, sømløs, banebrydende, revolutionerende, kraftfuld (as filler), ubesværet, omfattende (as filler), holistisk, synergi, uundværlig, afgørende (stacked), helt essentiel, i verdensklasse, næste niveau, game changer, lås op for, tag din rejse til næste niveau, dyk ned i, verden af, landskabet (unless literal), rejse (unless literal travel), magisk, uforglemmelig, drømmerejse, skjulte perler

## Corporate verbs
optimere (unless technical), facilitere, implementere, strømline, udnytte (as filler), sikre (stacked), muliggøre (as filler), gøre brug af

## English calques and anglicisms
- "i slutningen af dagen" (at the end of the day)
- "det er ingen hemmelighed at", "lad os være ærlige", "uden videre"
- "i nutidens hektiske verden", "i den digitale tidsalder"
- "uanset om du er nybegynder eller ekspert"
- "i denne artikel vil vi", "denne guide vil vise dig"
- "vil" stacked for simple future where Danish uses present tense ("du vil se" → "du ser")
- "gøre en forskel" as a filler hook; "tage et kig på" stacked; "bruge tid på at" fine, "spendere tid" not
- "giver mening" (calque of "makes sense") at most once; prefer "er fornuftigt" / "kan betale sig"
- særskrivning: "fly billet", "stor by ferie" (compounds are one word: flybillet, storbyferie)
- English words where Danish has a common one: "deals", "flights", "must-see", "hidden gem", "tips & tricks"
- Title Case in headings: Danish capitalises only the first word
- English word order with "ikke" in subordinate clauses ("hvis du vil ikke" → "hvis du ikke vil")

## Fake hooks
"Har du nogensinde undret dig...", "Hvad hvis jeg fortalte dig...", "Er du træt af...", "Leder du efter...?"

## ffly copy rules that bite in Danish
- never "ubegrænset" / "uden grænse"; never "garanti"/"garanteret" in a sentence with "billigst"/"lavest" (check-legal)
- never a word starting with "server" ("serverer" fails check-legal) or "database"
- no "Google"; say "en flysøgemaskine"
- write "du" (as every Danish travel site does)
